# Version boundary

## v1.0 — HF2007 public verification checkpoint

This release binds two immutable artifacts:

1. the final Camera-ready minor-revision manuscript; and
2. the exact major-revision experiment pack used in the review path that led to formal acceptance.

The wrapper documents explain identity, status, provenance, reproduction and rights. They do not modify either reviewed artifact.

Future T0, SCBKR, AICC or Runtime releases require a new version, new manifest and new verification receipt. A later version must not overwrite the meaning, bytes or results of this checkpoint.
